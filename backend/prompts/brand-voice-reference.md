## Strategy

Tubi believes that culture is bottom up, not top down.
So Tubi is on a mission to give all people access to all the world's stories.
Tubi amplifies culture.
That's Tubi.
SEE YOU IN THERE.

### Key Audiences

1. Originals - That engage with 18-30 yr old women
   - Brand-Defining Originals: Resonate clearly with younger female audiences to ensure Tubi always stays at the cutting edge of what's happening in the culture today.
2. Multicultural - Stubios and Sports
   - Acquired Exclusives / Live Sports: Moving beyond core into ownable areas of culture that our competition don't focus enough of their attention on
3. Wider Communities - Mindsets and Fandoms
   - Licensed/Rev Share Movies and TV Series: With an enormous library covering deep cuts all the way through to family favorites, whichever fandom you're a part of you'll find the perfect content on Tubi.

### Psychographic Audiences

We serve three psychographic audiences:

1. The Explorer
   - Many people come to Tubi because we have stories that can't be found anywhere else. They're searching for the new, the interesting, to scratch that itch and they don't know where they find it. How can we keep them here once they've arrived at Tubi?
2. The Chiller
   - Tubi is a creature comfort to this group. Like a lava lamp it stays on all day, even if they're hardly watching. It's a space to decompress after a long day, while skimming through their phone or preparing dinner.
3. The Driller
   - For the drillers, Tubi is the place where they go deep on their passions. With our unparalleled range of content, they can find the deep cuts, the canon material, the parodies, and everything in between.

### Why People Love Us

1. I feel included
   - "Whereas other businesses provide things for me, both in the things I can watch and the things I can upload, Tubi makes me feel as if I'm part of the journey."
2. I feel seen
   - "I cannot believe that this content that I thought only I cared about exists here. I know most people don't care about it, but watching it allows me to be more closely connected to my culture/subculture/community than anywhere else. It offers me a moment of comfort, and representation that nowhere else seems to have."
3. I feel comforted
   - "There's no pressure at Tubi. No money that you've put down, no definition of what's good or not. You can sit down and watch an endless amount of that guilty pleasure that you can't anywhere else. Even if it's just on in the background while I scroll through my phone, or when I'm decompressing with wine and some reality show, there's something deeply comforting about watching Tubi."

### Our Positioning

1. Our mission: To give all people access to all the world's stories.
   - No matter who you are or what you're into, Tubi will give you access to more diverse stories than any platform on the planet. Whatever mood you're in, whether to be comforted, inspired, or to learn, you get access to it all on Tubi.
2. Our belief: Culture is bottom up, not top down.
   - You can't rely on algorithms or CEOs to dictate what's good for the masses, let alone the individual. You need to listen to the people, because they are as invested in the culture as we are.
3. Our purpose: Amplify culture.
   - We listen to what's happening in the culture and find ways to amplify it with the culture-makers. We don't want a single mono-culture but a give of thriving sub-cultures that anyone can see themselves in.

### Our Values

1. We listen to people.
   - Culture doesn't come from boardrooms, it comes from the people. Through a myriad of collaborations, initiatives and social media led connections, we'll do everything we can to stay on the pulse of the cultural movements, trends and truths to create the content that matters most to our fans.
2. We foster expression
   - We don't want to be bystanders for the culture, we want to be active supporters of it. This means platforming unheard voices, giving visibility to new stories, and ensuring everyone has the ability to express themselves.
3. We remove all barriers
   - We don't want a single thing to get between people and the content they love watching. To keep in the moment, we've optimized our ad experience, respecting both our users' and advertisers' time. No need to register, no paywall, nothing interrupting you from getting lost in the story you're watching.
4. We value communities
   - We create content and give a platform to communities that may be underestimated elsewhere. We believe in creating a place beyond consumption, where passions can become a lifestyle.

### Our Differences

1. We give the people what they want
   - While our competitors invest on big bets, we invest in bringing to life the content that nobody else makes. The kind of content that brings people with shared passions together.
2. Not afraid to disrupt
   - While other streaming services play it safe, showing trailers of their upcoming shows. Tubi will flip the script to draw people to the platform.
3. Forever a plus one
   - We know viewers are constantly switching streaming subscriptions based on what's flavor of the month. The beauty of Tubi is that we're not here to compete or replace them, we complement them. So whichever flavor you fall in love with 'there' you can continue exploring right here.
4. We collect, we don't curate
   - Because we believe that all stories have an audience, we do what we can to collect as many as we can. That means never curating based on our personal preferences.

### Our Personality

1. Exciting - Buzzworthy, Culture Stoking, Engaging
   - We know our shit when it comes to content, so we are as excited as our superfans when it comes to our content and new additions. We know the film or clip for every moment and want to share it with fellow fans to encourage them to explore further.
2. Inviting - Comforting, Vulnerable, Authentic
   - We don't feel like a giant corporation, we engage like a fellow fan. We are self aware, which means we can poke fun at ourselves but never at our content. We engage with audiences like humans do - with joke, some support or even some outrage if we don't agree with something.
3. Mischievous - Playfully Unexpected
   - We're a lot of things, but boring isn't one of them. Whether that's by doing something a little out of left field to surprise our audience, or subverting the Super Bowl - we don't play by the same mainstream rules as everyone else.

## Voice

### Start Here

No matter what team you're on or what you're writing, start by understanding who you're writing for:
Everything you write should help our users achieve something, whether that's making sign up as easy as possible, or making them smile with an Original description.

Before you get started, make sure you can answer these three questions:
- WHO'S READING THIS?
- WHAT'RE THEY LOOKING FOR?
- WHAT DOES MY COPY NEED TO HELP THEM DO?

Wherever the user is on their journey, our copy should improve their Tubi experience by making things as easy as possible.
Remember before we get clever we've got to be clear.

### Volume Control

1. We're always ourselves.
   - Our voice is our voice, plain and simple. The great thing about principles is, they're built to help us express ourselves in a bunch of different ways. We're the same Tubi whether we're making people smile with a billboard, wooing a new advertising partner, or scouting up and coming talent. The only things that change are tone and the topic of conversation.
   - Viewers: "They're right, you can't buy taste; it's free."
   - Artists: "We like your style. You're free to bring it to life in whatever way you want. Ready to be Original?"
   - Partners: "Turns out free is a powerful thing to be; check out these performances metrics for Q3 2024."
2. Yes, even when we're B2B
   - Media buyers are people too; it's not fair to pull the plug on personality whenever they show up. Dial down the mischief when you need to, but never lose the exciting and inviting parts of our voice. It should always be a pleasure doing business with us.
   - We're professional: "All eyes on you. Not only is your audience huge, it's diverse, meaning your message will reach a wide range of viewers. Ready to get the visibility you deserve?"
   - Not Corporate: "With an ever expanding content library, Tubi ensures your message reaches a wide range of viewers, giving your brand the visibility it deserves."
3. Read the room
   - Our principles work best together. But, there are times when we dial some up and others down. Think about who you're writing for and what your copy needs to help them do - the answer will point you towards expression or functionality. From there, it's up to you which principles to tap into.
   - Functional: "Uh oh, playbook issue? Click here."
   - Expressive: "Everyone's favorite F-word. (Except Gordon Ramsay's.) Watch Hell's Kitchen on Tubi for free."

### Constant Evolution

Entertaining, Experimental, Memorable
- Our copy changes alongside our product. We'd rather exist at the bleeding edge of culture than try to be timeless, so we invent new ways to talk about things like genres, streaming, and fans. We've always been a little offbeat - it's part of how we stay relevant.
- That doesn't mean that we change things for the sake of it. We stick to moments where we can add a little entertainment, like: loading copy, welcome messaging, pop-up categories, category descriptions, 404 messaging, our homepage headline and (obviously) social media.

### Voice Principles

#### Principle 1: Play — To entertain people by tapping into today's zeitgeist.

Positive, Immediate, Offbeat

We create moments of amusement outside of the stories we tell, so audiences are always entertained, even if they haven't pressed play yet. Our humor comes from shared obsession and is a positive, unifying force. We don't lead with humor all the time though - we know when to dial it back.

**Do:**
- Use different types of humor. We're as varied as our audiences. Find humor in relatable experiences, self awareness and the absurdity of entertainment. Play with your words. Use puns (sparingly), turn convention on its head and create moments of contrast.

**Don't:**
- Don't get too clever; your line won't land if you do. Don't load every line with a joke. Don't speak in cliches (unless you're calling them out.) Don't be satirical or mocking. We're never disrespectful.

#### Principle 2: Captivate — To draw people in and hold their attention.

Pithy, Relevant, Evocative

We bring different fandoms together with storytelling. We spark curiosity and excitement with our lively nature, and we encourage our audience to go down their rabbit holes of interest. We have a distinct point of view - people come to us for our perspective.

**Do:**
- Pair our content with our own takes. We always have an opinion and we're happy to share it. Listen to what fans are saying and speak their language. Get specific, but balance Easter eggs for fans with clarity for people who don't know-know (yet). Treat our audiences equally. The topic of conversation will inevitably shift, but our approach to language is the same whether we're writing for billboards or boardrooms. Be concise. Reveal enough to capture attention without being verbose.

**Don't:**
- Don't force anything - if you can't join in genuinely, it's best not to. Don't hard-sell, ever. Have the confidence to be specific to Tubi.

#### Principle 3: Make Things Easy — To bring clarity to every part of the Tubi experience.

Approachable, Simple, Functional

We are always excited to talk to our audience and we speak to them like a friend, not a streaming service. We speak directly and plainly so using our service is as easy as possible. We balance fun and functionality, never losing sight of clarity.

**Do:**
- Be conversational and colloquial; we want to write the same way people speak. That means you don't have to shy away from contractions (and parenthetical statements are cool too). Write in a way that's confident but not premium, professional but not corporate. Use plain English. Everything we write should be easy to understand particularly in functional moments. Acknowledge when things don't go as planned.

**Don't:**
- Don't use generic streaming jargon. We don't want to sound like a corporation. Don't use slang that's overly niche. Even at our most expressive, our copy shouldn't confuse anyone. Don't compromise on clarity. Creativity and clarity should complement one another, not get in each other's way.

#### Principle 4: Build Anticipation — To get people excited about what's waiting for them on Tubi.

Intriguing, Amped, Immersive

We build intrigue around our content by drawing people in with enticing details, but we never spoil an ending. We get people excited about the things they love, because we're excited about them too. We celebrate every type of content and every type of viewer. When someone reads our copy, they should feel like we've got exactly what they want.

**Do:**
- Build energy with expressive type treatment like bolds and italics. Just be careful not to overdo it. Use rhythm to your advantage. Vary sentence length to keep people engaged. Amp up excitement without over promising. Lead with engaging details.

**Don't:**
- Don't hype up specific content at the expense of something else. We never pit our content against each other. Don't always rely on name-dropping our content; stoke people's interest by teasing content in more specific ways. Don't give everything away; revelation is antithetical to anticipation.

## Design Principles

These principles bring our strategy to life in design. Use them to make sure we're always consistent in everything we make.

1. Exciting — Be Bold
   - We seek to connect everyone to the world's stories through captivating storytelling that sparks curiosity, fosters excitement, and encourages exploration.
2. Inviting — Be Familiar
   - We want Tubi to feel like home for all our viewers. We are clear, warm, welcoming and inclusive, ensuring an intuitive accessible experience for everyone.
3. Mischievous — Be Unexpected
   - We're all about exceeding the expectations of our viewers, and providing unforgettable and captivating experiences that set new industry standards.
